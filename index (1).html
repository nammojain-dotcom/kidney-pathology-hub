Clinical Study & Review Guide

Glomerular disorders focus on the microscopic filtering membranes. Nephrotic Syndrome features heavy proteinuria (>3.5g/day), severe swelling (anasarca), and lipiduria, commonly driven by Minimal Change Disease or FSGS. Nephritic Syndrome features active sediment, hematuria (cola-colored urine), and hypertension, driven by immune-complex deposition in Glomerulonephritis.

For chronic progressive loss of renal function (CKD, Diabetic Nephropathy, Hypertensive Nephrosclerosis), treatment centers on efferent arteriolar vasodilation using ACE inhibitors / ARBs to reduce intraglomerular hydrostatic pressure, paired with SGLT2 inhibitors for tubuloglomerular feedback modulation.

Traditional approaches target systemic metabolic clearing (*Aama*) and doshic balance (*Pitta/Vata*). Ayurvedic modalities utilize *Rasayana* herbs (*Punarnava*, *Gokshura*), while Unani formulations focus on deobstruents (*Mofattet-e-Ashmari*) and cooling syrups (*Sharbat-e-Nilofar*).
